How to read an auction sheet

Grade
Grade & condition rating — the overall score above.
Score
Detailed 0–10 ratings for exterior, interior, engine, transmission, electrics — like a MOT on each system.
Mileage
Odometer reading (in km) — we check it against the documented history.
Specs
Engine, transmission, drivetrain, colour and equipment (sunroof, navigation, etc.).
Inspector notes
Scratches, dents, repairs noted by the inspector — the honest bits other sites skip.

We share the full auction sheet with you BEFORE you bid — and attach it to your order as evidence. If we ever see a grade we wouldn't recommend, we say so.
